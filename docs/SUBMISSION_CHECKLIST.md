# FieldShift — final Space Apps submission checklist

**Challenge:** [Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/field-shift-adapting-farms-with-nasa-data/)

**Project:** FieldShift

**Live project:** https://fieldshift-nasa.streamlit.app/
**Source code:** https://github.com/sagar25446-prog/Nasa-Hacakathon

The official 2026 guide says project submission closes at **11:59 p.m. local time on Sunday, November 15, 2026**. The team must submit before the deadline shown for its chosen Local or Universal Event.

## Required before “Submit for Judging”

- [ ] Every team member appears as a confirmed participant at the same Local or Universal Event.
- [ ] Every required field on the **Project** tab is complete and in English.
- [ ] Project name: **FieldShift**.
- [ ] High-level summary and detailed description pasted from `docs/PROJECT_SUBMISSION_CONTENT.md`.
- [ ] The NASA POWER source is named specifically in the NASA Data field.
- [ ] The complete references are pasted from `docs/SUBMISSION_REFERENCES.md`.
- [ ] The SI/AI answer is checked and updated for every tool used by every team member.
- [ ] A **public** project-demo link is pasted. It must be either a video of 30 seconds or less or a deck of no more than seven slides, including title and introduction slides.
- [ ] The project-demo link opens in a private browser without a password, permission request, registration, or login.
- [ ] The public final-project link points to https://fieldshift-nasa.streamlit.app/ and opens successfully.
- [ ] The live app has been checked through the Punjab / Ludhiana demonstration flow.
- [ ] Every image, video, audio asset, data source, and software dependency used in the submission is cited or has permission for use.
- [ ] The demo contains no child likenesses, no NASA logos or branding elements, and no NASA endorsement claim.
- [ ] Team members agree to the Terms and Conditions and confirm the project is original.

## Best submission order

1. Record the 30-second demo using `docs/DEMO_VIDEO_SCRIPT.md`.
2. Upload the video to a public host and test it in a private browser.
3. Copy the exact text from `docs/PROJECT_SUBMISSION_CONTENT.md` into the Project tab.
4. Paste the public demo link and live application link.
5. Paste the references and complete the NASA-data and SI/AI fields.
6. Save progress. Ask every team member to check the saved project page.
7. Select **Submit for Judging**. Confirm the success banner. The guide says the team may continue editing until the event ends, but judges review the last saved version.

## Last safety checks

- Do not supply video or document files directly to the Space Apps form; the guide accepts only image uploads there and asks for public external links to demo and project materials.
- Do not state that the app provides locally validated recommendations outside the supported pilot catalogues.
- Do not state that climate context directly measures soil pH, NPK, organic carbon, or yield.
- Do not state that stress-test results are forecasts.
