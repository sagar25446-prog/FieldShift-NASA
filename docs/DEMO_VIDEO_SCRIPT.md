# 🎬 FieldShift — 75-Second Competition Demo Video Script
**Target Duration:** 75 Seconds (1:15) | **Format:** Screen Recording + Voiceover Narration

---

### [00:00 – 00:12] The Problem: Sinking Aquifers & Monoculture Vulnerability
- **Visual:** Open with satellite imagery of Northwestern India from NASA GRACE showing red groundwater loss contours, cutting to a view of the FieldShift Home Page with the headline banner and Ludhiana coordinates.
- **Voiceover (Fast, urgent, confident):**
  > "Across the world's breadbaskets, farmers are trapped in monocultures that drain groundwater and deplete soils. In Punjab, India, NASA GRACE satellites show aquifers sinking at four centimeters every single year. Farmers want to diversify, but they lack clear, science-backed guidance on water, soil, and climate risk. Meet FieldShift."

---

### [00:12 – 00:26] The Solution & Farm Input
- **Visual:** Navigate to **Farm Profile** tab. Click "Load Demonstration Preset (Ludhiana)". Show the tubewell water budget calculator auto-calculating 800 mm water budget from pump horsepower and hours.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "FieldShift turns NASA Earth observation data into hyper-local crop rotation decisions. A farmer enters their location, soil conditions, and water capacity—using our intuitive tubewell calculator. FieldShift instantly pulls historical solar radiation, temperature, and precipitation from NASA POWER anywhere on Earth."

---

### [00:26 – 00:43] The Recommendation Engine & Pareto Optimization
- **Visual:** Switch to **Rotation Recommendations**. Point to the headline banner: *"Conserves 1,000 mm of net irrigation (10,000 m³/ha) vs Rice-Wheat baseline"*. Expand Plan 1 (*Maize &rarr; Wheat &rarr; Summer Moong*). Scroll through the Economics tab showing GoI MSP, gross margins, and FAO-56 Penman-Monteith water balance.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Instead of black-box machine learning, our engine performs transparent Pareto multi-objective optimization across peer-reviewed university practice guides. It delivers candidate rotations ranked by water suitability, soil recovery, and diversity. Look at Plan 1: shifting to Maize, Wheat, and Summer Moong saves 10 million litres of irrigation water per hectare, with verified Government MSP procurement support."

---

### [00:43 – 00:58] Stress-Testing Future Climate with NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6
- **Visual:** Click on **Future Climate (NASA CMIP6)**. Show the multi-model ensemble spread for 2041–2060 under SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5. Point to the extreme heat threshold warnings.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Will these rotations survive mid-century heat? FieldShift stress-tests candidate plans against downscaled NASA CMIP6 projections. If March temperatures breach anthesis thresholds, the system alerts the farmer before seeds are in the ground."

---

### [00:58 – 01:15] Global Scalability & Printable Summary Card
- **Visual:** Switch the Region dropdown to **US Corn Belt (Iowa)** and **Mediterranean (Spain)** to show global adaptiveness. Click **Export 1-Page Summary Card** to reveal the formatted printable farmer card. Show the footer with 247+ passed tests.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "FieldShift isn't just for Punjab. Our modular architecture supports the US Corn Belt, Mediterranean drylands, and semi-arid tropics worldwide. With one click, extension workers can export a one-page summary card for the field. Tested, open-source, and powered by NASA Earth observations: FieldShift is how we shift fields toward resilience."
