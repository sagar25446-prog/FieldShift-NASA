# FieldShift — seven-slide project-demo deck

`submission/FieldShift_Project_Demo_Deck.pptx` is the official-format alternative to the 30-second video. It has exactly seven slides, including its title slide, and keeps the same accurate claims as the live application.

## Slide order

1. **FieldShift** — Team name, project name, and one-sentence purpose.
2. **The farming decision** — Why temperature, rainfall, water, soil, and crop history make rotation planning difficult.
3. **Farm context** — The farmer provides location, soil and irrigation information, prior crops, and priorities; unknowns stay visible.
4. **NASA climate context** — Historical NASA POWER daily data, provenance, units, dates, and gridded-data limit.
5. **Rotation comparison** — Deterministic sequences, hard feasibility checks, transparent component metrics, and current-rotation comparison.
6. **Stress tests and uncertainty** — Dry, hot, and reduced-irrigation hypothetical tests; uncertainty categories and farmer control.
7. **Impact and boundaries** — What FieldShift enables, what it does not claim, and the live-project URL.
